About us

Moving India, one trip at a time.

Vahan360 is built by CORE LINK COMMUNICATION PVT LTD — a Bengaluru company that knows logistics from the ground up.

Our story

CORE LINK COMMUNICATION PVT LTD was founded in 2025 in Bengaluru with a simple belief: moving goods and people should be reliable, transparent and fair — for customers and for the drivers who make it happen.

We started in logistics and goods transport. Vahan360 is the next step — bringing that experience into one intelligent app for everyone.
